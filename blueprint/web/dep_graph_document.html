Definition 1Faces of the unit sphere

For a compact space \(X\), a point \(x\in X\), and \(\varepsilon \in \mathbb R\), define

\[ F(x,\varepsilon )=\{ f\in C(X,\mathbb R):\| f\| =1\text{ and }f(x)=\varepsilon \} . \]
